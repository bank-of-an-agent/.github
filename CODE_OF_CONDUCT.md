# Code of conduct

We adopt the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/)
as our code of conduct for every space the Bank of an Agent organization manages: repositories,
issues, pull requests, discussions and events.

In short: be respectful and constructive, assume good intent, and keep discussion focused on
the work. Harassment and personal attacks are not tolerated.

Report unacceptable behavior privately to the organization maintainers. Reports are handled
confidentially, and maintainers who are involved in a report recuse themselves.
