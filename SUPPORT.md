# Getting help

- **Questions and ideas:** open a discussion or an issue in the most relevant repository
  ([agent-bank](https://github.com/bank-of-an-agent/agent-bank) if unsure).
- **Bugs:** use the bug report template; include versions, chain (Base Sepolia or local) and a
  minimal reproduction.
- **Security problems:** never in public. See [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).
- **Lost access or Agent Bank unavailable:** your funds stay in your own smart account. Use the
  [exit tool](https://github.com/bank-of-an-agent/exit-tool) with your owner key.
